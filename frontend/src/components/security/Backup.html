Temenos SaaS Backup and Restore

Database Backup

After Go-live, DB backups of Class A & Class B Environments only;

other environments on an ad-hoc basis subject to additional fees.

System Backups

After Go-live, DB backups of Class A & Class B Environments only

at the end of every calendar month and year

Restoration

Class A Environment only

  • Point-In-Time-Restore (PITR): restored to any 5-minute restore point in time within the rolling retention period
  • Transact, TPH : a rolling 14*-day retention period
  • All other products: rolling 7 day retention period

Other environments on an ad-hoc basis subject to additional fees.

Backup Long Term Retention

Applies to Class A Environment only

  • EOM backups retention period = 24 month
  • EOY backups retention period = 7 year

Extended retention periods are subject to additional fees.
